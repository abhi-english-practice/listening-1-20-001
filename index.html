POP DOSSIER IELTS COLLOCATION LISTENING

1. Face the consequences

IPA: /feɪs ðə ˈkɒnsɪkwənsɪz/
Meaning: accept and deal with the results of an action
Vietnamese: đối mặt với hậu quả
Example: If we ignore climate problems, we may face the consequences.

2. Make a significant contribution

IPA: /meɪk ə sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt ˌkɒntrɪˈbjuːʃən/
Meaning: do something important that helps a situation
Vietnamese: đóng góp đáng kể
Example: Everyone can make a significant contribution to protecting nature.

3. Play a vital role

IPA: /pleɪ ə ˈvaɪtl rəʊl/
Meaning: be extremely important in a process
Vietnamese: đóng vai trò quan trọng
Example: Technology plays a vital role in modern education.

4. Raise public awareness

IPA: /reɪz ˈpʌblɪk əˈweənəs/
Meaning: help people understand an issue
Vietnamese: nâng cao nhận thức cộng đồng
Example: Campaigns can raise public awareness of pollution.

5. Take effective measures

IPA: /teɪk ɪˈfektɪv ˈmeʒəz/
Meaning: use successful actions to solve a problem
Vietnamese: thực hiện biện pháp hiệu quả
Example: Governments should take effective measures to reduce waste.

6. Achieve long-term goals

IPA: /əˈtʃiːv ˌlɒŋ ˈtɜːm ɡəʊlz/
Meaning: reach important aims over time
Vietnamese: đạt mục tiêu dài hạn
Example: Students need discipline to achieve long-term goals.

7. Develop critical thinking

IPA: /dɪˈveləp ˈkrɪtɪkl ˈθɪŋkɪŋ/
Meaning: learn to analyse information carefully
Vietnamese: phát triển tư duy phản biện
Example: Reading helps students develop critical thinking.

8. Gain valuable experience

IPA: /ɡeɪn ˈvæljuəbl ɪkˈspɪəriəns/
Meaning: get useful knowledge from activities
Vietnamese: có kinh nghiệm quý giá
Example: Internships help young people gain valuable experience.

9. Overcome major challenges

IPA: /ˌəʊvəˈkʌm ˈmeɪdʒə ˈtʃælɪndʒɪz/
Meaning: successfully deal with difficult situations
Vietnamese: vượt qua thử thách lớn
Example: People must overcome major challenges in life.

10. Maintain a healthy lifestyle

IPA: /meɪnˈteɪn ə ˈhelθi ˈlaɪfstaɪl/
Meaning: keep habits that support good health
Vietnamese: duy trì lối sống lành mạnh
Example: Exercise helps people maintain a healthy lifestyle.

11. Acquire new skills

IPA: /əˈkwaɪə njuː skɪlz/
Meaning: learn new abilities
Vietnamese: tiếp thu kỹ năng mới
Example: Students can acquire new skills through practice.

12. Expand knowledge

IPA: /ɪkˈspænd ˈnɒlɪdʒ/
Meaning: increase understanding
Vietnamese: mở rộng kiến thức
Example: Travelling can expand our knowledge.

13. Reduce environmental impact

IPA: /rɪˈdjuːs ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmentl ˈɪmpækt/
Meaning: make harmful effects on nature smaller
Vietnamese: giảm tác động môi trường
Example: Recycling can reduce environmental impact.

14. Preserve cultural heritage

IPA: /prɪˈzɜːv ˈkʌltʃərəl ˈherɪtɪdʒ/
Meaning: protect traditions and historical values
Vietnamese: bảo tồn di sản văn hoá
Example: Local communities preserve cultural heritage.

15. Build strong relationships

IPA: /bɪld strɒŋ rɪˈleɪʃənʃɪps/
Meaning: create good connections with people
Vietnamese: xây dựng mối quan hệ bền chặt
Example: Team activities build strong relationships.

16. Achieve academic success

IPA: /əˈtʃiːv ˌækəˈdemɪk səkˈses/
Meaning: do well in education
Vietnamese: đạt thành công học tập
Example: Hard work is necessary to achieve academic success.

17. Make informed decisions

IPA: /meɪk ɪnˈfɔːmd dɪˈsɪʒənz/
Meaning: choose after understanding information
Vietnamese: đưa ra quyết định có hiểu biết
Example: People should make informed decisions.

18. Provide equal opportunities

IPA: /prəˈvaɪd ˈiːkwəl ˌɒpəˈtjuːnətiz/
Meaning: give the same chances to everyone
Vietnamese: cung cấp cơ hội bình đẳng
Example: Schools should provide equal opportunities.

19. Create positive changes

IPA: /kriˈeɪt ˈpɒzətɪv ˈtʃeɪndʒɪz/
Meaning: make improvements
Vietnamese: tạo ra thay đổi tích cực
Example: Small actions can create positive changes.

20. Meet social expectations

IPA: /miːt ˈsəʊʃəl ˌekspekˈteɪʃənz/
Meaning: satisfy what society expects
